## W52 empirical feasibility probe

Use the [BioImage Archive 2026 migration account](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag1108) as the single primary case. It documents conversion by bioformats2raw to OME-Zarr, image-level discovery restricted to converted images at the reported stage, and retained deposited source files. Its June 30 2026 snapshot and planned coverage milestones are author-reported, and give no individual conversion dates or counterfactual assignment; repository-wide rollout, searchability and depositor/analyst choice are distinct. Treat this as a real non-AI comparator with a known indexing rule, not AI-caused entrenchment. Use the [independent S-BIAD602 audit](https://doi.org/10.1111/jmi.70125) and [Gehrels et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2214205120) to identify one concrete figure/test and its necessary raw inputs. Keep separate source files never deposited, files available by accession, files discoverable through image search, and data converted sufficiently for the test. The independent audit attributes obstacles to incompleteness of deposited data and documentation; no link from its failure to the archive's format policy has been established. No assumptions about this individual accession's conversion state.

One source audit across the case: ledger indexed by accession/object, date/format, deposit versus converted discovery, raw and derived paths, real restrictions and budget, actor/decision, figure/endpoint criteria, independent reanalysis outcome. Require a credible timing/eligibility contrast before treating a before-after comparison as causal; conversion selection may reflect modality, readiness or demand. If verifying availability only, report feasibility. No new large benchmark as a substitute. For an AI-specific extension, check Long FAIRkit for a dated AI deployment tied to observed research-group choices and a genuine downstream test on the same chain. Keep that as a limited evidence query and do not combine it with BIA into a single causal effect. Direct fidelity loss is an ordinary data/measurement rival; the actual change in route use and validation requires distinct evidence.

## Reassessment after the bounded probe

- Actual test, primary artifact and traceable source-route/usage change, plus credible separation from deposition, format loss and budget: continue focused case research. If AI treatment/uptake cannot be identified, qualify the study as non-AI evidence about institutional access.
- Measurable change only in indexing, discovery, convenience or accuracy, or effective raw-route recovery at comparable cost: keep as a mechanism-specific technical contrast, recommend transferring the replay tools to Research OS.
- No candidate with traceable endpoint/use evidence or no observable prediction beyond existing accounts: recommend retiring the independent P77 line for now. Reopen on genuinely new evidence. Do not close the Lab or erase records through this decision.
- [Liu et al. 2011](https://doi.org/10.1287/isre.1090.0255) and [Liu et al. 2012](https://aisel.aisnet.org/misq/vol36/iss3/14/) are serious rivals to novelty and directional universality. Test choice changing because a research institution changes workflow validation or access is the remaining candidate, with standard economics, data journeys and ordinary measurement/availability as explicit competitors.
