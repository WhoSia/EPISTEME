## 2. The paper's six rows were matched to the workbook's exact task design

All 36 recall values (two repositories × six rows × three methods) in `overall tissue` matched the corresponding cells in the repository-specific sheets: ovarian/ovary, liver/liver, lung/lung, followed by the blood query for each of the same three cancer strata. The original Methods describe 3 × 800 records per repository, 4,800 total. Appendix C says six nonoverlapping 800-record sub-cohorts **per** repository, or 4,800 per repository. The workbook evidence points to six query results, consistent with reuse of a cancer corpus for two queries. Actual accession-level overlap, including potential across-cancer and study-level dependence, awaits the original archive. The sheet `geo-gpt4` labels its method rows `Llama3 + DD/CEDAR` (there is a distinct `Geo-llama3` sheet with different values). The model identity of the numbers is unresolved pending a run receipt.

Recalculated using the Appendix C described balanced row-block ANOVA (`recall ~ method + row`) and Friedman with tie correction on the published cell values. [Executed Python](../analysis/p77_w50_recheck.py), standard library, embedded numeric transcriptions. The equal-weight workbook means pooled across 12 query rows give baseline 17.651995% and CEDAR 62.875572% from workbook aggregate cells, consistent with the reported headline after the paper's display convention. They are query averages, not accession-weighted sensitivity.

BioSample's statistic is computationally reproduced; its reference p-value still relies on the design assumptions. In the author reply and [Reviewer 3's Revision 1 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/instance/13108262/bin/giag019_reviewer_3_report_revision_1.pdf), the reviewer explicitly requested assumptions, error bars and post-hoc correction. The mismatch is already present in [Revision 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/instance/13108262/bin/giag019_giga-d-25-00224_revision_2.pdf) and persisted to the corrected published version. The Revision 1 and Revision 2 full texts and Reviewer 2/3 follow-ups were retrieved for this scope; the entire peer-review packet was not archived.

Sensitivity: equally average the two query results within each cancer stratum. CEDAR-minus-baseline differences (ovary, liver, lung) were BioSample +47.09, +81.90, +55.05 percentage points and GEO +21.79, −12.40, +77.90 pp. A two-sided three-block sign-flip check gives .25 and .50 under sign exchangeability; model-based stratum-blocked F(2,4) gives p=.00283 and .21162. With three blocks the smallest two-sided sign-flip p is .25. The difference between inferential conventions and unknown accession overlap limits what either sensitivity analysis establishes. This is no basis for a corrected formal population-level p-value.

## 4. Which comparison is now worth running

Use [Bernstein, Doan & Dewey 2017, MetaSRA](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btx334) as the domain-relevant non-LLM competitor for BioSample tissue retrieval. Its Text Reasoning Graph tracks origin and distinguishes mention from assertion, with scoped lexical/fuzzy matching and rule-based filtering. [Ikeda et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1093/gigascience/giaf070) already uses a direct MetaSRA baseline for its own cell-line endpoint and makes a separate benchmark public. Neither establishes a result on Sundaram's identical tissue records. A minimal matched pilot requires the same accession list, source fields and ontology release, comparable reviewer time and training examples, query context and abstention rules. Freeze deduplication by accession/study and reference independently with blinded reviewers using the source record plus corroborating documents and explicit unknowns; the authors' tissue labels are a replication target, not the sole adjudicator. Record precision/recall and phenotype or sample-definition discordance separately. For the ADNI schema pilot use versioned deterministic dictionary/Usagi-style matching with reviewer time parity, alongside a source-aware raw route that records actual cost, permissions and accepted review.

For founding TLP, a worked symptom-code check is a positive control for detecting semantic reversals, not a scientific result. Choose a real published rival-hypothesis pair and fix an admissibility rule, minimal analyzable cohort, and raw/derived participant/visit definition in advance. Track standard chosen, actual tests attempted, failed, excluded or accepted, and which raw route an investigator could have used under the same conditions. The decisive comparison is whether two regimes with matched technical fidelity and comparable time have different peer-level access to a scientifically valid test. If an accessible raw path or ordinary mapping/phenotype misclassification accounts for the difference, that observation does not establish an independent EPISTEME explanation. Existing OMOP research already measures and corrects phenotype error; consult [OHDSI QBA empirical and synthetic evaluation](https://data.ohdsi.org/QbaEvaluation/) before attributing a downstream effect change to standards entrenchment.
