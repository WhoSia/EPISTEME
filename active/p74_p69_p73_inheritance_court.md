## 3. What P74 actually inherits as a causal structure

Represent separate variables: original recorded source content `B_t`; publishing/revoking authority `A_t`; role/referent `R_t`; announced audit-notice `N_t`; actual audit acquisition `C_t`; evaluator interface/contract `I_t`; observed model/human verdict `J_t`; independent warrant acceptance `W_t`; eventual representation-switch action `D_t`. External processes and source clusters may influence all these.

**Crucial non-equivalences**
1. A legitimate authority interval for an *observed* document is not a certificate about the **unobserved potential outcome under no notice**.
2. A model output effect under output-contract manipulation (P69) is not an intervention effect on an institution's mutable source (P74).
3. An AVeriTeC web URL embedded in dataset bytes is not evidence that the remote page was verified at the relevant calendar time (P70/P73).
4. A preservation or change in a score is neither independent human semantic judgment nor source authority (P69–P72).
5. Even a sign-identified notice-effect under *explicit hypothetical crossworld assumptions* does not prove institutional switching should occur; this would require warrant and policy evaluation.

A possible future causal DAG requires explicit exposure versions and may not identify all arrows without real interventions. The inherited pieces constrain the design, not the direction or magnitude of unknown mechanisms.

## 4. P74-P1 — New executable separation test

[Exact Python](../src/p74_p1_authority_identification_court.py). Synthetic witness `(source_id, hash, role, referent, authority interval [start,end), revocation time, issuer, collector, observed time)`. Validates:
- temporal interval direction / UTC aware time, issuer and collector separate from synthetic owner, source-hash equality, role/referent continuity, no retrospective future attestation, explicit revocation epoch; fail closed on any purported `synthetic_only=False` external-certification promotion.
- 8 synthetic source units: **7 valid and 1 revoked as of observation**, purely toy typed-authority classification.
- Original 256 missing-potential-outcome completions yield ACE [-1/4,+3/4]. Adding these synthetic **observed-world authority records leaves all 256 and full ACE interval unchanged**. This is a negative-control test against illicit evidence promotion.
- Only explicitly entering an additional `HYPOTHETICAL_CROSSWORLD_STRUCTURAL_ASSUMPTION` may prune potential worlds. Two of four treated units assumed to have Y(0)=0: 64 completions, ACE [0,+3/4]; all four so assumed: 16 completions, ACE [+1/4,+3/4]. This narrowing is paid for by a **strong unverified structural premise**, NOT a discovery from the authority records.
- Negative tests reject mismatched publisher/capture hashes, fake external authority, future-signed snapshots, role/referent change, expired/reversed intervals, duplicate sources, unauthorized constraints.

**Inference authority:** these are finite toy calculations. They do not establish genuine independent signatures, original content truth, intervention noninterference, population transport, meaningful human warrant or scholarly novelty. The P74-P1 abstract operator is a standard identified-set intersection. Authority changes only address the *authority-admissibility component of evidence roles* in these fixtures; full epistemic warrant remains withheld.
