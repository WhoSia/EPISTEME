### 3.1 Restricted grammar and original-source-inspired counterexamples

One atom `(source_id, revision, effective_from, effective_until, record_time, subject, predicate, object, direct_claim/direct_defeater, polarity, full_text_presence, excerpt_presence)`. A decision at valid time `t` and record cutoff `r` accepts an atom only if effective, known by `r`, has identical explicit claim referent, and is actually available in full source. Two contradictory atoms -> `SOURCE_RELATIVE_CONFLICT`, neither -> `UNDETERMINED`. This is **restricted literal logic**, not a validated full natural-language semantic parser.

Four executed fixtures, inspired by actual P75-P4 original-source observations:
- historical nickname removed from snippet but **persisting elsewhere in the source** at a new version -> latest snippet wrongly sees loss; full source correctly retains witness;
- source assertion with contemporaneous editorial objection -> a newer source claim does not yield unopposed evidence;
- similar surface claim with different quantifier/referent -> do not infer an exact claim from a wrong-scope fact;
- claim with `effective_from=t` observed only at later `record_time` and former source atom `effective_until=t` -> no anticipatory future record or revival of **expired source**.

**Important qualification:** These are source-**inspired finite fixtures**; their atom-level role labels inherit assistant interpretation, not independent world truth. The actual 12 source lineages had been verified previously; P5 does not pretend to rerun full original licensed raw text inside this new 6-minute offline CI.

### 3.3 Robust conditional switching value, not an observed human gain

Assumed uncertain world type has `p = P(new representation truly better)`, restricted to an author-specified interval `p in [l,u]`. Stipulated transition cost `k` and a hypothetical perfectly informative additional **machine/physical observation**, available or unavailable, with declared cost `r`.

Defined loss functions, for `0<=k<=1`:
- `L(KEEP,p)=p`;
- `L(SWITCH,p)=1-p+k`;
- `L(TEST,p)=r+p*k`, **only if perfect additional information is genuinely available under the assumed model**. TEST is an information acquisition operation, **not recruitment of a human reviewer**. It must not be interpreted as an actual service with known accuracy, and is unavailable when not posited.

Minimize worst-case expected losses over the uncertainty interval. Five predeclared intervals × three transition costs × three hypothetical test costs × two test-availability states = **90** finite exact conditions. Python `fractions.Fraction` and independent Go scaled integer by 1600 match **90/90**, including **34** grid cells where opening the hypothetical informative test option changes the set of robustly minimizing actions and **11** cells with tied minimizers. At `[l,u]=[0,1]`, `k=1/4`, `r=1/8`: absent a test `KEEP` worst loss 1, and with the perfect-test assumption `TEST` worst loss `3/8`. This is a conditional decision calculation, **NOT measured decision behavior, benefit, or a new information-value theorem**.

**Identification boundary:** Two unobserved world-truth assignments can share all original signed/hashed Wikipedia sources and real version metadata but require opposite actual representation switches; an independent original page alone cannot identify true migration payoff. The simple bound `[-1,+1]` in a stipulated unit-loss binary countermodel is a general partial-identification illustration, **not empirical P75 sample-based welfare estimation**. A stronger rival equipped with equally informative observations and standard decision theory can match these finite decisions.

## 5. Return to original EPISTEME founding question and P76 name proposal

Lab founder's question, from Notion root: **When globally interoperable, digitally standardized, AI-mediated knowledge infrastructures become entrenched, what determines whether superior representations, measurements, ontologies, standards and theories can actually replace them?**

The P75 closure shows that validating source edition, claim scope and conditional decision logic is necessary but **not the explanation of entrenched infrastructure transitions**. The next frontier is *endogenous loss of the capacity to distinguish rivals at all* when installed knowledge standards alter which experiments, data and sources survive.

**EPISTEME-P76 — Endogenous Defeat-Capacity Lock-in & the Irreversibility of Epistemic Migration: Representation-Dependent Verification Options, Evidence-Production Hysteresis, Restoration Thresholds & Cross-Infrastructure Rival Falsification**
