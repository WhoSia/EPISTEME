## 0. Scientific conclusion
The 2000 LEP closure gives a *source-attested event* in which an institution chose not to continue a historically operable experimental setup despite a contemporary request for more data. It **does not** prove the closure suppressed a genuine 115 GeV Higgs discovery or that the scientific alternative ceased to be testable elsewhere. Distinguish **new data acquisition**, **existing-data reanalysis**, **alternative-instrument testing**, and **theory-level warrant**. These pathways have different states and may evolve in different directions.

## 2. Three traps disproved by these primary sources

**Trap A — Institutional closure = epistemic annihilation.** LEP shut down physically, but 2001–03 published data reanalysis remained possible. Other machines (Tevatron, LHC) were planned or eventually available. The *apparatus-specific acquisition opportunity* was foreclosed, not all possible tests and not the existing data's inferential value.

**Trap B — Five-sigma discovery was lost in 2000.** A 5σ prospect assumed a ~115 GeV real signal, limited resources and detector assumptions; the 2003 combination fell to 1.7σ, and the later Higgs had ~125 GeV mass. Never transform a counterfactual conditional forecast into a historical fact.

**Trap C — Hindsight retroactively validates a 2000 decision.** Later 125 GeV discovery and final 1.7σ cannot determine whether the 2000 choice maximized ex-ante expected scientific value under competing programmes. Need contemporaneous joint probabilities, uncertainty on costs/delays, scientific opportunity valuation and opportunity costs. The primary records give some constraints, but NOT a defensible unique optimum.

## 3. Conceptual model: four separate pathways, no discovery claimed

At time t define an evidence-capacity state E_t=(N_t,R_t,X_t,W_t):
- N: ability to acquire *new samples* with focal apparatus LEP.
- R: ability to *reanalyze recorded samples* (software, calibration, archived data).
- X: ability to test related hypotheses with *alternative/new apparatus*.
- W: warranted strength of *specific* claim under the evidence available at t.

2000-11-08: N after 2000 ends. But R remains active, as 2003 publication proves. X is not necessarily zero (Tevatron / later LHC). W changed with data interpretation (from preliminary 2.9σ to final 1.7σ for 115 GeV). This separation is an **analytical bookkeeping aid**, not an original theory of evidential warrant. Need account for paths competing under finite funding and mutually exclusive tunnel access.

The *capacity* to collect fresh LEP events was lost; this did not eliminate all ability to criticize the Higgs claim. Also, source time must be indexed: evidence revision over time must not leak into the ex-ante reconstruction of 2000 decisions.

## 4. Historical specificity of physical infrastructure
- LEP and LHC shared the same tunnel; LEP dismantling and subsequent LHC modifications were on the director's critical path.
- [Maiani 2017](https://indico.cern.ch/event/517784/contributions/2482178/attachments/1462700/2259801/LHCP2017_2.pdf) reports early plans to coexist, abandoned by ~1995. The 4m tunnel diameter initially enabled later LHC reuse, while eventual replacement blocked extending LEP. This is an interesting two-sided *enabling and limiting* history, but Maiani himself already made the point; no novelty on that alone.

## 6a. Independent archival corroboration and a more demanding philosophical interpretation

[CERN Archives, LEP fonds](https://archives.web.cern.ch/CERN_archive/guide/accelerators/LEP/isalep/) expressly states that, after LEP ceased operation, LEP data analysis continued with possibility of finding new phenomena. Its finding aids identify surviving LEP/LEPC minutes and management records. This validates the distinction between acquisition shutdown and inference shutdown as an **original archival authority**, not just the inference drawn by EPISTEME.

The archive lists potentially retrievable contemporaneous LEPC and management committee minutes. These may be materially stronger for reconstructing ex-ante deliberation than later memoirs or generic newspaper reports; exact minutes have NOT yet been retrieved or cross-examined in this study. No claim of full archival coverage.

The same tunnel gave multiple competing science options at different times: initial LEP infrastructure allowed subsequent LHC, but the LHC installation in the shared tunnel constrained LEP continuation. Maiani's own records describe both sides. Neither path-independent freedom nor universal lock-in is supported.

**Research gate:** Prior to independent interpretive claims, examine contemporaneous LEPC / RB minutes and ask (a) what extension schedule was actually viable, (b) what subjective signal likelihood and resource uncertainty existed, (c) whether option constraints were physical, contractual or contingent, and (d) whether any **strong rival historical account** misses a concrete primary-source fact. Avoid retrospective success-story bias.
