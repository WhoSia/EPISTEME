# EPISTEME P76 — Writing Loop W5: Discourse-Conditional Editing Reversal (Preregistered Pilot)

2026-10-20 KST. Design and candidate pre-screen only. NO human-reader outcomes collected. P76 OPEN; P77 HOLD; PAPER HOLD.

## Founding-question audit
This is an EPISTEME **measurement/control probe**, not proof that historical scientific infrastructure locks in knowledge. The scientific analogy is query-relative adequacy: preservation of propositional content does not guarantee an utterance is equally usable by a reader in all discourse contexts. The known language-science rivals are information structure, givenness, focus, and referential accessibility, so a context interaction is expected by prior art. Distinguish linguistic research from P76 core causal proposition; do not relabel familiar givenness effects as P77 novelty.

## Predeclared experiment
Unit: (context, claim, editing operation) packet; randomization at participant and item within matched contexts. Conditions: C1 given-continuation, C2 newly introduced referent, C3 correction/contrastive focus, C4 rival-hypothesis adjudication. For each meaning-locked underlying proposition build original O and edit E(O) with exactly one frozen operation, applied **without retuning the rule by context**. Control lexical content as much as possible.

Operations:
- E1: replace repeated full technical noun phrase on second mention with an anaphoric demonstrative/short reference. MUST preserve unique reference and avoid adding false givenness; prospective source of semantic-gate exclusions.
- E2: front a previously mentioned topical phrase (without changing logical scope or evidential hedges); if fronting introduces focus presupposition or contrast, reject.
- E3: expand a compressed abstract noun phrase into an explicit relative clause without changing agent/negation/modality.
- E4: replace a repeated technical noun phrase with a non-anaphoric precise synonym **only if extension demonstrably matches**; semantic drift common.

## Frozen semantic gate (before reader test)
Two independent reviewers blind to any hypothesized winner each inspect the full context AND two candidate variants.
Hard rejection if:
(1) changed referent/antecedent, including multiple possible demonstrative antecedents;
(2) negation, quantifier, modal strength or scope changes;
(3) altered source/provenance/uncertainty or citation attachment;
(4) changed presupposition, contrast implication or question-under-discussion resolution important to scientific interpretation;
(5) changed temporal, causal or conditional relation;
(6) omission of a rival/hypothesis or numerical qualification.
Annotate meaning equivalence as PASS/FAIL/UNCERTAIN; UNCERTAIN is excluded from primary comparison. Reviewers disagree => adjudication blinded to reader outcomes, or HOLD. Technical claim and measurement assumptions must be preserved. Formal bidirectional entailment is useful but does not suffice for discourse meaning. Candidate examples below are NOT independently human-cleared.

## Reader study (not executed)
Use context+sentence pairs as stimuli. Blinded variant labels, counterbalanced order via Latin-square across items and participants, with washout/filler. Separate outcome axes: (i) comprehension questions with objectively checked referents and inference, (ii) clarity 1–7, (iii) ease of locating evidence provenance 1–7, (iv) preferred wording, (v) reading time only if reliable instrumentation. Require predeclared reader eligibility and avoid presenting AI-generated responses as humans. Primary contrast is edit minus original within each context, and **reversal** requires opposite signed estimated effects across contexts with appropriate uncertainty, not anecdotes or arbitrary scoring. Mixed effects: outcome ~ edit * context + (1+edit|participant) + (1|item), simplifying random effects only on justified convergence criteria. No sample size or statistical power claim until pilot variance data.

Risk: a semantic PASS that is obtained by per-context wording repair changes E into different interventions. Report intention-to-edit eligibility for all originally constructed packets and selection/attrition by context. If semantic pass rate differs markedly by context, avoid generalizing crossover to all possible texts.

## Concrete pre-screen packets, not reader outcomes

Packet A — E1 full NP to demonstrative:
C1: "The archived calibration ledger was digitized yesterday. The archived calibration ledger lists the original correction coefficients." → "The archived calibration ledger was digitized yesterday. **That ledger** lists the original correction coefficients."
C1 provisional gate: LIKELY PASS; referent uniquely established.
C2: "Two ledgers were recovered: an archive ledger and a technician's ledger. The archived calibration ledger lists the original correction coefficients." → "... **That ledger** lists the original correction coefficients."
C2 provisional gate: FAIL/UNCERTAIN (multiple antecedents). Exclude, do NOT recruit this pair as an apparent negative effect.

Packet B — E3 abstract NP to relative clause:
C1: "Independent logs recorded when the detector ran. The uncertainty in the calibration procedure remains unresolved." → "Independent logs recorded when the detector ran. **How the calibration procedure was uncertain** remains unresolved."
Gate: FAIL: paraphrase changes grammatical sense/meaning. Retain as semantic-gate rejection illustration, not reader trial.

Packet C — E1 within scientific claim, controlled context:
C1: "Two archives exist. The HDF4 archive is the older one. The HDF4 archive contains two shared monitor objects." → "Two archives exist. The HDF4 archive is the older one. **That archive** contains two shared monitor objects."
Gate: LIKELY PASS under unique reference.
C3: "Was it the HDF4 archive or the HDF5 archive that had two shared monitor objects? The HDF4 archive contains two shared monitor objects." → "... **That archive** contains two shared monitor objects."
Gate: UNCERTAIN due to multiple active antecedents and contrast. Exclude without guessing preference.

Packet D — E3 with explicit agent:
C1: "The team verified the raw checksum. Its verification of the archived numeric arrays remains incomplete." → "The team verified the raw checksum. **The team has not finished verifying the archived numeric arrays.**"
Gate: UNCERTAIN: 'remains incomplete' and 'has not finished' temporal/aspectual implicatures may diverge. Exclude.

## What success would / would not mean
If future independent readers show a context×edit sign crossover after both variants clear semantic review, it establishes bounded contextual editing effects. Information-structure literature anticipated interactions of discourse and wording. It does not establish P76's adoption-caused scientific defeat route, nor P77 novelty. If only semantic failures produce apparent reversal, that is an invalid manipulation, not reader heterogeneity.
