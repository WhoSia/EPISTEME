## Main defeat findings
- Leonelli 2009 ties data, phenomena, local context and the basis of warranted claims; proposal that data have no fixed query-independent evidential meaning is directly anticipated.
- Leonelli 2014 explicitly argues important data-science difference lies not merely in quantity but in infrastructure, curation, technologies, expertise and knowledge. Kill naive 'infrastructure influences evidence' novelty.
- Bechhofer et al. 2013 explicitly argues Linked Data publication alone is not enough: provenance, quality, attribution, methods and Research Objects for reproducibility. Kill 'file bytes or linked resources alone insufficient' novelty.
- Peng 2011 presents computational reproducibility as a minimum standard distinct from independent replication. Kill 'runnable code not full scientific replication' as novel proposition.
- Borgman et al. 2014 studies the rise/fall of knowledge infrastructures, data management and domain-specific expertise. Kill generalized knowledge-infrastructure historical lock-in novelty.
- Borgman 2015 book details science-specific data infrastructure, social and institutional context; general sociotechnical coupling is preempted.
- DPHEP 2025 and especially DPHEP 2026 provide direct high-energy-physics archival reanalysis casework. 2026 report has explicit sections on ALEPH advanced jet flavour tagging; DELPHI legacy conversion to EDM4HEP and a new repository; OPAL 26-year-later reanalysis; JADE open data release and CERNLIB restoration; real loss/retention caveats. This is strong source ecology and prospective defeat of treating 'resurrecting LEP science' as our independent discovery.
