## New independently validated distinctions

**A. Embryo note anchors an acquisition clock, not yet published panel membership.** The actual RTF states `#2 t0 = frame 20`, `30 seconds per frame`, `Pixel size 0.49 um`. The first two agree exactly with W53's `t0=20` and `0.5 min/frame`, but the note does **not explicitly prove that this WT embryo was included in Figure 2E's 5 analyzed WT specimens** or that its `t0` annotation was assigned using precisely the paper's biological `Tcell=0` operation. A shared number does not certify identical biological registration. Figure 2E is a genotype-by-embryo comparison, not one embryo at four geometry settings. W53/W54 do not identify the production per-embryo t0, contour orientation, author smoothing parameters or excluded tracks.

**B. Spatial calibration is a small, *different* sensitivity axis.** W53 used author demo `0.497 µm/pixel`; the actual per-day deposit note reports `0.49 µm/pixel` (may be rounded). In the published W53 replay script, this coefficient multiplies PIV-projected velocity *after* its calculation; replacing only this scale therefore multiplies W53 velocity and every difference by **0.49/0.497 = 0.985915493**, or **−1.40845%**. Since the factor is positive, it preserves sign. It does not change W54's 30-s index-offset reversal. This is a conditional algebraic re-expression under the replay code, not a fresh re-run of the PIV pipeline, nor a verdict on the authors' precise calibration.

**C. A real source/key join distinguishes discoverability from missing raw source.** The stored index query has **5** objects with 5 unique file patterns. Exactly **3** belong to W53's candidate WT `embryo1_210122_2`: a full membrane TIFF, a full myosin TIFF, and only the **`MARKERS/F_020.tif`** contour marker. The required **`MARKERS/F_038.tif`** and **`F_054.tif`** have no corresponding hit **in this archived response**, despite their original source bytes being retained by the W53 source ZIP and actual PIV having been calculated using the deposited original path. Other two hits are one cropped Lightsheet WT from another embryo, and one toll-vl frame, not the remaining 16 source samples. This tests **archived query inclusion only**, not the entire BIA live search, the exact underlying ingest reason, a lost original file, or downstream adoption.

**D. An observed discrete stress-test envelope can cross zero without being a confidence interval.** Recomputed `Δ = v(17 min)−v(9 min)` from W54's three *common-time-index* offsets `−1, 0, +1` for each of four alternative direction/reference settings, where 1 step is 30 seconds. Each setting's discrete min/max spans zero:
- D-cw/Q-4 `[−0.481235,+0.320836]` µm/min
- D-cw/Q-2 `[−0.401483,+0.262743]`
- D-ccw/Q-4 `[−0.478857,+0.294583]`
- D-ccw/Q-2 `[−0.412469,+0.228914]`.
The 30s later minus central difference ranges from `−0.589709` to `−0.720691` µm/min. By comparison W54's **held-contour** negative control puts the maximum mask-selection difference at `0.025722` µm/min in the tested settings. These are not calibrated probabilities for t0 uncertainty, physical velocity confidence intervals, or four biological replicates. The original RTF actually gives an explicit t0; artificially shifting it is an analytic stress test, not proof t0 is wrong. Nor can we infer which configuration produced the publication panel from a sign reversal.
