## Interpretations (NOT discoveries)
Strongest empirical EPISTEME negative result candidate: a global representation-effect geometry did not transfer to fresh tasks, while some operator-level reversals occurred. But mere failure-to-transport is not novel against new interaction/decomposition literature and cannot identify mechanism without independent artifact checks.
Promising methodology question: establish a prediction *abstention criterion* for representation effects on new tasks, with calibrated uncertainty over transfer and scoring provenance. Counterargument: uncertainty and selective prediction are longstanding, with evidence sufficiency benchmarks as recent rivals.
Alternative research use: create a rigorous failure atlas explaining why tempting summaries of EPISTEME's P-sequence overclaim historical results. That is valuable internally without being a new scientific theory.
