## Scope and scientific observable

[Gehrels et al. 2023, Figure 2E](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9963527/) reports the spatially averaged tangential tissue velocity over time for 5 wildtype, 6 toll vl, and 5 concertina (cta) embryos. It compares 9 and 17 min via two-tailed unpaired t-tests. [Original supplement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/instance/9963527/bin/pnas.2214205120.sapp.pdf) identifies the segmentation and PIV procedures. Figure 2E measures an observable used to argue for distinct early/late flow drivers; it does not by itself settle the full geometry-versus-adhesion account. Scope remains one panel, not a new interpretation of the biology.

[Ambroset et al. 2026, §2.1.2/§3.1–3.3](https://doi.org/10.1111/jmi.70125) independently described one wildtype two-photon embryo with 78 times for both channels, one mask, and one toll vl membrane frame. It also described one lightsheet frame, demo movies and software-environment barriers. Its [supporting material](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/instance/13686567/bin/JMI-304-101-s001.pdf) reproduces the directory tree examined. That report is a historical observation of the accessible material then. Do not carry its file-availability judgment over to a newer snapshot.
