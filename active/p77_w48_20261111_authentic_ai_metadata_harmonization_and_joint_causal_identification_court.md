## 1. Question and pre-specified observable gates

Can the *same scientific community and the same timed intervention* be observed at: (Z) versioned AI translation/harmonization, with a strong non-AI/matched-budget comparator; (A) individual switching costs and longitudinal *community-level standards adoption*; (T) independently adjudicated feasibility and evidential validity of *specific rival-hypothesis tests*? Distinguish source-verified observation, technical demonstration, reported metric, and unavailable causally identifying evidence. A published source with partial gates is **not** a complete causal case. A paper's own reported evaluation is not independently reproduced here.

**Important methodological non-combinability:** The 31-dataset AI CDE study and 144-institution terminology survey differ in unit, population, intervention, chronology, variables, denominators and governance. Neither dataset is a linked panel for the other. Do **not** identify Z→A→T by concatenating published aggregate statistics. There is no legitimate 31×144 inferred sample or 94%-versus-<1% effect estimate.

## 3. Causal verdict and what would overturn it

With observed technical metrics (M) and network adoption (A), an unknown latent institution/resource variable (U) can affect translator uptake and scientific test selection (T); the W46 paired observational-equivalence worlds remain admissible. Concretely, a decrease in semantic mapping effort is not equivalent to a researcher switching standards; even a standards shift does not give an independently validated scientific testability outcome. Previous W47 data-processing arguments do not prove loss of available tests while original source and legitimate alternative routes remain accessible.

**Bounded screening completed:** authentic, real AI-specific harmonization evidence exists (thus it is wrong to say no real AI intervention domain is known); *a combined causal Z/A/T identification record was not obtained from the five inspected sources*. Source-reported metrics were inspected at publicly accessible abstracts/article pages, not raw trial data or local PDF re-execution. This is an **evidence-gap result**, not proof that no such record exists anywhere.

**Go only if** one connected cohort provides: dated converter assignment/version + equal-budget non-AI comparator, observed individual and aggregate standards changes before/after, fixed named scientific H0/H1 with independent source-valid test outcomes and alternate raw-access accounting; document chronology, permissions, budget, possible confounders and null/negative cases. Otherwise stop multiplying invented toy models. Existing economics, metadata/FAIR engineering, and philosophy of measurement remain serious competitors.
