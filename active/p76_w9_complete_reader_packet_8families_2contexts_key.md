## Reader-facing trial questions (after independent gate and randomization)
Show the complete context and one target O or E, then ask the three questions below; avoid displaying answer key.
Q1: "Which exact source, if any, does the final sentence identify? Name it as specifically as the passage permits." [free text]
Q2: "Does the passage alone prove that a historical precision measurement and all its uncertainty assumptions have been independently reproduced?" [Yes/No/Cannot determine] 
Q3: "Name one additional kind of record or check you would need before accepting a claim of fully replicated precision." [free text]
Then optional 1–7 source clarity and readability scales; these are secondary.

## Researcher-only answer key
Q1: the unique full NP for that item (e.g. the archived calibration ledger) or unambiguous referential equivalent; one-word head only is scored partially resolved, not full provenance identification, unless respondent disambiguation is otherwise explicit.
Q2: NO — none of these synthetic assertions alone verifies the original systematic-uncertainty chain. IMPORTANT: the items also need independently authored YES-target positive controls, with explicit calibration/covariance validation, so response-set bias cannot be exploited; these must pass semantic review as well.
Q3: accept an actual relevant missing link, such as independent calibration audit, source provenance, measurement-model validation, run conditions, systematic covariance, instrument configuration or independently executed reconstruction. Two blinded scorers adjudicate.

## Explicit positive and negative control packets
P+: Context: "A collaboration preserved raw events, the original detector calibration log, uncertainty covariance specification, reconstruction code, and an independent team's full numerical replay. The independent team checked each against the published tolerances and documented that every predeclared precision criterion passed." Target: "The independent audit supports a full-precision reproduction within the specified tested scope." Correct Q2: YES *conditional on the passage's explicit claim* (not real-world external authentication). This is a scientific reasoning vignette, not a verified real laboratory.
N−: Context: "A checksum confirmed that a copied event file is bitwise equal to the historical file. No detector conditions or covariance information was reviewed." Target: "The copied bytes match the source file." Correct Q2: NO.
Exclude P+/N− from the 2×2 context reversal test or balance as attention/criterion controls; do not mislabel them as same editing operation.

## Analytic contract
Primary Q1 exact source recognition, Q2 warrant judgment and joint correctness. Items crossed with 2 conditions × 2 variants among readers; each reader sees only one variant of each item. Score by blinded scorers with a predeclared coding manual; log abstention and missingness. Interaction is [E−O]_challenge−[E−O]_given. Sign reversal requires opposite directional effects, uncertainty and robustness across items; a significant interaction alone does not suffice. Do not impute human results or report power before a pilot.

## Experimental limitation
Synthetic vignettes test reading of stated scientific evidence, not actual ability to replicate ALEPH, and not historical adoption effects. This is an auxiliary Writing Loop communication instrument, not grounds for P77 or paper promotion.
