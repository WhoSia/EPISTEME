## 3. Necessary inference correction: FOUR mechanism families do not imply an inferential sample of 8,192

Under an additional **sign-symmetry reference model**, four genuinely independent family-level signs permit only 16 sign assignments; hence the smallest two-sided reference probability is 2/16 = **0.125** and the smallest one-sided is 1/16 = **0.0625**. Even unanimously positive four-family evidence cannot pass a 5% two-sided sign-symmetry threshold. These are NOT design-based randomization p-values because task-family signs were not randomly allocated. The exact-support calculation is a sensitivity diagnostic.

For at least six independent families, that two-sided resolution can be 2/64 = **0.03125** if every sign agrees. Six is a mathematical lower bound for resolution in that stylized procedure, **not** a sufficiency or power guarantee. Eight synthetic task variants from four authored families cannot be declared eight independent clusters to lower a p-value.

The original LOFO Brier uses held-out task anchor vertices to calibrate its task intercept, then tests non-anchor vertices. To avoid even potential within-invocation calibration leakage, an additional predeclared sensitivity uses **only held-out A draws 1–4 for calibration, and independent B draw labels 5–8 for non-anchor evaluation**. Each candidate transport predictor and task-only baseline uses identical anchors. Draws are **invocation replicates, not transmitted provider seeds**. Neither synthetic fixture computes a behavioral result.

**Non-transport cannot be established by rejecting no hypotheses.** Prospective prediction improvement, practically justified margins, uncertainty over truly independent mechanisms, and explicit model/provider family breakdown must support any nontransport claim.
