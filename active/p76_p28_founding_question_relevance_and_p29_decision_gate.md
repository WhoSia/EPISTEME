## Mandatory long-horizon reminder

Founding EPISTEME question (September 18, 2026): when standardized, networked, translated and AI-mediated knowledge infrastructures become entrenched, do they determine whether a superior rival can be detected, seriously tested and adopted? Infrastructure can serve as memory, prison or migration scaffold. The point is *not* HDF4 versus HDF5 as a format benchmark.

Original proposed Translation-Layer Paradox (compatibility both alleviates and potentially accelerates lock-in) was anticipated by standards/converters research. P1 broad switchability/legibility and P2 broad theory-laden evidence generation were also reduced against prior art. P3 generic maintenance/irreversibility belongs substantially to Forand and research-option scholarship. P18 failed its recoverable witness positive control. P57–P59 showed why local representation effects need not transport. P69–P75 forced a distinction between retained content, source credibility, decision authority and measured human/world consequences.

## Stage-to-founding traceability for current experiment

1. P27/P28 **file bytes and values**: establishes source custody and query-specific invariants. It is necessary measurement work, but cannot itself establish historical epistemic lock-in.
2. **Object graph/alias difference**: makes a preservation claim falsifiable for one class of storage-based queries; strongly anticipated by NeXus/ETL and pseudoreplication. This is a control, not a publishable new principle.
3. **Acquisition-unit semantics**: needs independent original LRMECS DAQ/run logs and PLS interpretation; physical interpretation is *not* inferred by matching arrays, content hashes or object links.
4. **Historical institutional migration/adoption**: needs a documented transition from an incumbent scientific representation to a replacement, with actors/decisions and evidence-production capabilities before/after. A documentation tutorial and companion archive do not count.
5. **Causal contrast and strong rival**: discriminate lost future scientific tests due to adopted infrastructure from ordinary file copying, ETL, missing provenance, software/tool unavailability, option-maintenance economics and instrument retirement.
6. **Warranted consequence**: demonstrate one scientifically authorized discriminatory question whose answer, availability or justified adoption differs. A contrived unique-object sum is not such a consequence.
