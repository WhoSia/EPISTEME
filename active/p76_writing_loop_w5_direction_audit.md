# EPISTEME — W5 direction and P77 novelty audit

2026-10-20 KST. Methodological steering decision, not an empirical outcome.

## Scope boundary
EPISTEME's founding research question is about installed knowledge infrastructures shaping **feasible discriminatory scientific tests** and eventual rival adoption. Contextual editing may be relevant as a component of representational legibility and a Writing Loop quality check, but a generic prose-preference experiment is a *different research program*. It is not legitimate to claim a meaningful EPISTEME P76 achievement merely from sign reversal in clarity scores.

## Four independent claim gates
A: semantic preservation of same science proposition/uncertainty/provenance; necessary for valid edit contrasts.
B: reader understanding improvement and context interaction; independently measured, presently UNOBSERVED.
C: science-workflow consequence (retrieval of evidence, detection of invalid inference, ability to specify an admissible falsifier); only if demonstrated with task-grounded measures.
D: historical institutional adoption changing future test capability; requires adoption counterfactual and strong rival discrimination.
A does not imply B; B does not imply C; C does not imply D. No stage is allowed to be laundered into later-stage evidence.

## Prior-art/rival audit
Discourse givenness, focus, referential accessibility, and newness are established predictors of processing, hence a context×editing-operation crossover may be an ordinary instantiation, not a new theory. Counterexamples: referential ambiguity, pragmatic focus shift, logical-scope distortion, and item selection bias can mimic reader preference reversal. Semantic gate should *reduce* but not simply hide these cases; report pass/fail counts by discourse condition.

## Research progression
Track A/B as a separate Writing Loop methods evaluation; do not displace P28 native-HDF4 verification and its unauthenticated CI, P29 historical JADE/ALEPH/DELPHI source-to-query studies, or Paper 3 causal identification. Next upgrade only if a controlled edit influences the correctness of actual scientific criticism across representations, and then only as a limited study of task-conditioned legibility. P77 is NOT opened on wording manipulation alone.

## Writing quality rubric
For each candidate sentence maintain scientific claim ledger: proposition, uncertainty, agent, source, contrast, question under discussion and presupposition. Prefer coherent natural prose and purposeful rhythm; avoid mechanical uniformly shorter sentences or automatic synonym replacement. Record precise before→after edits and explicit rejection grounds rather than praise or a global 'naturalness' score.
