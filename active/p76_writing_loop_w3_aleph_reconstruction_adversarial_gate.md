## Newly identified strong counterpressure: ALEPH Z-lineshape reconstruction
The official ALEPH Collaboration policy approved 2003-12-04 explicitly says some analyses, including Z lineshape and W mass, cannot be repeated on archived data, particularly those needing systematic-error information:
https://dphep.web.cern.ch/sites/default/files/archive_data.pdf

A public independent project dated June 2026 (jfc-mit/analysis_aleph_z_lineshape) attempts reconstruction of Z lineshape; its retrieval log tracks original primary ALEPH Z resonance publication, per-energy luminosities, and systematic uncertainty corrections and documents row-shift errors discovered in its own extracted luminosity mapping:
https://github.com/jfc-mit/analysis_aleph_z_lineshape/blob/master/retrieval_log.md

The existence of this project is NOT proof of a successful independently authenticated original-precision replication. This is a falsifier candidate for broad 'impossible' language. Compare (a) digitized publication fit targets; (b) archived-event numerical reproduction; (c) independently recovered detector and beam systematic covariances; (d) authority and statistical fidelity of scientific claim. Without (c), fitted parameter proximity is not equivalent to original measurement re-execution.

Read the published original ALEPH paper, *Measurement of the Z resonance parameters at LEP*, EPJ C 14 (2000), CERN report EP-99-104, directly; independent project logs are source tracing aids and fallible. Prior to any success claim, check project main README, commit versions, actual tests/logs/artifacts, error budget, sample coverage, per-energy luminosity row mapping and current status.
