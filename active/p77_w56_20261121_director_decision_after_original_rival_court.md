## Specific adjudicated overlap with established research
1. **Representation, conversion and technical cost**: Moore et al. (2021) already distinguish transient translation from permanent conversion, explain conditional direct-access advantages of NGFF and countervailing costs (many small chunk files, single-file portability and serving stacks), and recommend complementarity of TIFF/HDF5/Zarr. The later BIA original/OME-Zarr access contrast is an application of this documented workflow, not independently novel science epistemology. The 2021 Figure 1 is source-authentic synthetic access-performance evidence, not downstream biological warrant.
2. **Metadata semantics and discovery**: Gonçalves & Musen (2019) already give a large observed old snapshot (NCBI 6,615,347 plus EBI 4,793,915 metadata records, **not unique disjoint sample count**) and measure field name/value specification violations. Their study also identifies limits of purely edit-distance-based clustering. Current LLM-assistance reports must beat this established non-AI diagnosis on fixed contemporary inputs. Comparing 2017 source records with 2026 experiments is not a controlled time-series intervention.
3. **Scientific test feasibility**: W53 source and derived image pixels and PIV values agreed for fixed inputs; W54 a 30-second frame shift changed the sign of the local 17min–9min contrast under some choices. This demonstrates sensitivity to analysis choices, *not* that the representation changed biology or that an institution foreclosed the test. Original access paths were still available. This is a **Research OS technical reproducibility result**.
4. **Prior-art defeat test:** All plausible general statements from these observations ('format changes access', 'metadata nonuniformity hurts retrieval', 'scientific workflows depend on an existing representation') have strong source-authenticated rivals. Rename them as independent EPISTEME mechanisms only if a distinct empirical residual is demonstrated under those rivals.
