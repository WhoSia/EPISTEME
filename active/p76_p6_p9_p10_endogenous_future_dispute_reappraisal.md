## 1. What P9 actually proved (and what it did not)

For history h, future dispute d and restoration action r with value V_d(r,h), define equivalence h ~_D h' if all pairwise restoration rankings agree for every d in fixed D:
sign[V_d(r1,h)-V_d(r2,h)] = sign[V_d(r1,h')-V_d(r2,h')] for all r1,r2.
Then H/~_D is the coarsest EXACT quotient sufficient for **that declared comparison signature**. Expanding fixed task family D weakly refines this equivalence. This is elementary decision sufficiency, not novel EPISTEME math.

**P76 correction:** Ranking equality is *not* equivalent to preservation of numeric regret, exact cost, or costed feasible paths. Example: difference between actions 1 versus 0, and 100 versus 0 yields identical rank but different maximum regret. P76-P5's cost-labelled bisimulation sufficiency is stronger than P9 decision-order equivalence. No claim that a task-specific regret-optimal quotient is identical to the coarsest strong bisimulation; this resembles EvoNOMOS repair-cost sufficient quotient hierarchy.

For truly open and unconstrained future tasks, any two distinguishable histories might be separated by a later dispute, so lossless retention may be required to guarantee **universal** future rankings. This is a conditional almost-trivial separation statement, **not** empirical fact or new universal theorem. It is uninformative unless future task generation is structurally constrained.

## 2. The P10 feedback that P76-P5's path model can miss

## 4. Constructed minimal counterexample plus neutralizing rival

Single source history contains distinguished bit z; q_R retains z; q_C erases z; both retain bit x=0. There are two questions:
- d_x, generated under both q_C,q_R, for which incumbent answer remains consistent;
- d_z, admissible under q_R (can refer to historical bit z) but not constructible under q_C, and for which incumbent's claim is contradicted by the preserved old value z=1.
The coarse policy has 0 internal audit failures by construction while the richer policy reveals 1 counterexample. This does **not** by itself distinguish P76 from Goodhart/selection bias or classical provenance. It is only a diagnostic witness for how endogenous question-set sampling can manufacture internal “PASS”.

**Counterexample to P10's overly casual monotonic intuition:** A richer policy could introduce a competing new instrument that replaces some old tasks rather than simply adding them, hence D(q_C)⊆D(q_R) is an **assumption**, not universal. Models can also forget or reallocate resources, and grant different rights. Do not write monotone regret conclusions without nesting and common loss functions.
