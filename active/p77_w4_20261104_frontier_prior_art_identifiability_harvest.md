## New Harvest seeds (none promoted)
**H1: Warrant-Identifiability Under Pipeline Multiplicity.** Define a fixed physical estimand theta and admissible pipeline family P with genuinely independent calibration/testimony. For every p ∈ P produce a measurement estimate and coverage-calibrated uncertainty set I_p; determine whether a scientific comparison D(H0,H1) is *identifiable* across P. Nontrivial if externally valid observational constraints and choices distinguish multiple plausible pipelines, not merely extremal intervals. Prior-art fatal rival: Zhan et al. 2026 framework, partial identification/robustness and experimental design.
**H2: Claim-Expiry Certificates Under Source-Pipeline Revision.** Treat revisions to calibration, provenance or source withdrawal as *specified interventions* that may alter permissible scientific claims while file values remain unchanged. Measure claim revision vs baseline retrieval recency/evidence authority and prior art on temporal knowledge; no claim of novel concept.
**H3: Evaluator-Induced Epistemic Ranking Reversal.** Audit whether a scientific ability ordering changes when scoring provenance, judge semantics, and source independence are corrected. Hard rivals: Bean 2025, General scales Nature 2026, Hua 2025, correlation mirage EMNLP 2026. Methodology-only candidate.

## One falsifiable task (only after reading all direct prior art)
Source authentic two-pipeline public science case with fixed hypothesis discrimination and ground-truth reference from an independent measurement/experiment. Freeze pipeline family and validity requirements prospectively. Compare (i) naive single-pipeline conclusion, (ii) pipeline sensitivity, (iii) source-qualified partial identification, (iv) evidence-linked end-to-end audit. Primary success = independently validated change in a legitimately warranted discrimination decision, not simply changed output score. Failure = both methods same or changes lack independent physical warrant. Threats: pipeline choice endogeneity, artifacts, calibration uncertainty, source selection, equivalent data reused. If prior work already does this test, withdraw independent originality.
