# EPISTEME P76 — P29 candidate source ecology: JADE revival versus DELPHI preservation limits

Date: 2026-10-18 KST. This is a source-based PRIORITY SCREEN, not P29 formally opened, no causal discovery and no paper promotion.

## Founding-question binding
EPISTEME asks when historical knowledge infrastructure enables or forecloses discrimination of superior scientific rivals, rather than whether a file migration can alter object graph. The decisive estimand is a counterfactual future verification route conditional on archive/software/custody/adoption history, not HDF4-to-HDF5 checksum differences.

## Candidate A: JADE PETRA revival and fragile custody
Historical 2008 workshop report: https://www.ariadne.ac.uk/issue/58/dplta-hep-rpt/ describes JADE data/software revival and a per-run/per-fill recorded-luminosity file lost from an individual's DESY account after archive cleanup, with an old ASCII printout subsequently found. This specific rescue suggests scientific reconstruction may depend on outside-the-official-archive evidence and preserved human expertise. The report is an account of an earlier talk, not a certified complete causal reconstruction. Investigate original Bethke presentation and subsequent JADE reanalysis papers, what luminosity metadata was actually recovered, and which precise cross-period QCD questions depended on it. A lucky recovery is a possible falsifier of irreversibility, not proof of permanent route extinction.

## Candidate B: DELPHI experiment
DELPHI official preservation policy of March 12, 2024: https://dphep.web.cern.ch/delphi-preservation-policy/ says some precision results increasingly difficult to reproduce because detailed detector and run-conditions expertise is disappearing. It discourages repeat high-precision analysis even while retaining SHORT DST, RAW data, documentation and software. Distinguish preserved nominal bits from executable, expert-authorized conditions for reproducibility. Verify whether specific analyses actually cannot be repeated, which calibration or human expertise is missing, and whether software modernization reopens routes. Policy statements alone are NOT measured impossibility.

## Strongest counterpressure / direct prior art
DPHEP's 2009 report https://lss.fnal.gov/archive/2009/pub/fermilab-pub-09-856-cd.pdf already warned that raw HEP data and analysis capabilities can disappear with changing hardware/software and expertise. CERN LHC open data policy https://opendata.cern.ch/docs/cern-open-data-policy-for-lhc-experiments and DPHEP's 2026 report https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06775 cover sustainable preservation and successful legacy reanalysis. Ordinary data preservation and socio-technical infrastructure loss are not new EPISTEME theory. Preservation SUCCESS is a meaningful rival and must be tested.

## Research design: distinguish four outcomes
(1) historically valid source survives and a rival-sensitive analysis is independently executable;
(2) source bytes survive but calibrated analysis cannot currently execute;
(3) original archive access lost but independent witness permits bounded reconstruction;
(4) source and recoverable witness missing, leaving a specific question unidentified. Only with documented adoption and counterfactual contrast can outcomes motivate EPISTEME-specific adoption-induced loss of epistemic defeat capacity.

## Evidence acquisition, not assertion
Obtain original archival policy revisions, raw/derived data custody lists, run/calibration logs, environment images and notebooks, published reanalysis scripts, actual reanalysis output. Define one historically meaningful rival scientific hypothesis and preregister feasible query before comparing cohorts. Competing explanations include ordinary missingness, personnel departure, storage migrations, and available alternative measurements. No claim of measured causal entrenchment until independently authenticated.

## Relative prioritization
JADE provides unusually specific rescue/witness evidence; DELPHI provides documented institutional warning and positive preservation controls. These are more connected to EPISTEME's founding question than repeating LRMECS format conversion.
