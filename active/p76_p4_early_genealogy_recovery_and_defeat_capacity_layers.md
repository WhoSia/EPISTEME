## 2. Minimal defensible decomposition

For a rival-target proposition and a fixed factual epoch, distinguish **R retention of a witness**, **A operational availability/access of the witness to an evaluator or executable**, and **G successful actual criticism generation**. Furthermore distinguish **I** interpretation of a supplied criticism and **X** execution/falsification of the original assertion after criticism is available.

A proposed defeat-capacity transition may selectively change R, A, G, I or X, with different interventions and identifiable signatures:

- `R=0`: original evidence atoms/relations absent in all retained archival records (must establish source inventory, not just one view).
- `R=1,A=0`: source retained but old API, rights, reader or toolchain prevents feasible access under declared budget; may be reversible.
- `R=1,A=1,G=0`: available true witness but criticism generator fails; P18 recovered-witness failure means this can happen without actual loss of source bytes.
- `R=1,A=1,G=1,I=0`: generator outputs a criticism but model cannot interpret its referent or relevance.
- `R=1,A=1,G=1,I=1,X=0`: criticism understood but falsification test itself cannot execute (missing physical instrument or procedure).

These five are *operational diagnostic classes*, not a new theorem; the R/A/G/I/X decomposition itself is strongly inherited from P11–P18. Never conflate a binary 'defeat capacity' metric with these stages unless equivalence is separately verified.

## 3. What makes P76-P4 a real scientific improvement over P76-P1

The official NASA HDF4/HDF5 original reader/converter experiment should separate:
1. Original scientific datum available in source: `R`.
2. Legacy HDF4 reader and converter executable at bounded runtime and licensing cost: `A`.
3. Query correctly constructs target discrepancy/falsification witness with source mapping: `G`.
4. Independent parser/proof role validator assigns correct target referent and time: `I`.
5. Actual original-vs-converted scientific query and archived reader oracle reruns: `X`.

This prevents accidental promotion of **format incompatibility** (an A- or G-layer issue), **lost data** (R), **misinterpreted evidence** (I), or **failed physical observation** (X) into a novel causal claim about adoption-induced verification extinction. **Strong rivals survive:** Forand irreversible maintained/discarded options; HDF Group task-relative compatibility and exact object mapping; PROV/bitemporal original-source access; standard software execution feasibility; neural-output-channel observation effects.
