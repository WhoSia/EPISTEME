## Killer prior art re-read

DPHEP Collaboration, *Data preservation in high energy physics*, European Physical Journal C 83 (2023), DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-023-11885-1, explicitly defines preservation as maintaining the ability to extract new science, not just retaining bits. Its taxonomy contains four use-case levels, ranging from publication metadata to whole reconstruction/simulation chains. It names software, documentation, organization, tacit knowledge and operating conditions as necessary preservation objects and reports successful returns from preservation programs. This is DIRECT prior art against a claim that EPISTEME invented 'bytes are not enough', query-relativity, or executable capacity.

The 2026 DPHEP global report arXiv:2607.06775 also explicitly documents successful legacy revival, automation, continued institutional stewardship and sustainability limitations. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06775 . Any EPISTEME contribution must demonstrate a causal/dynamic or new falsifiable observation that the preservation framework does not already capture, not merely restate this state of practice.

Therefore require *specific adoption intervention and control*, a predeclared rival-discriminating observable, and proof of why rival theories cannot explain the historical contrast.
