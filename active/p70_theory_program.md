## Explanandum and EPISTEME origin return

What determines whether a representation, measurement scheme, ontology or knowledge infrastructure can *replace* an entrenched alternative across contexts? The recent LLM representation experiments supply one local, falsifiable test bed: do task-mechanism descriptions permit reliable predictions of what a supposedly semantics-preserving representation intervention will do outside the environments where it was observed? A positive result would support **conditional switching guidance**, not a universal law of institutional adoption. A negative result should distinguish absent invariants from insufficient support and from instrumentation/format selection.

## Four logically distinct objects

1. **Semantic edit:** On ecology e, an operator O_(e,o): X_e -> X_e is valid only if the task's truth answer and a bijective inclusion-minimal proof-witness family are preserved. Maintaining just the answer is insufficient.
2. **Operator bridge:** A mechanism map phi: X_s -> X_t must commute with the named intervention at the chosen structural quotient: phi(O_(s,o)(x)) equivalent to O_(t,o)(phi(x)). This is a refutable engineering condition, not a general fact about renaming, trace order, or record order.
3. **Measured outcome:** Y_(e,b,c)(o) is the 0/1 *all-call executable correctness* of model/provider bundle b under output contract c. An invalid or missing response gives Y=0 for execution, but its **latent semantic correctness remains unknown**. Also report separately: provider response, format validity, and correctness conditional on valid format. The 2026-10-09 P69 pilot demonstrates why channel c must remain in the index.
4. **Conditional effect:** tau_(e,b,c)(m,o)=E[Y(o)-Y(I) | mechanism m, ecology e, bundle b, contract c]. Differences are intervention-attributed only within randomized, oracle-invariant, paired designs. The model+provider bundle cannot identify a pure model-family effect.

## Theorem P70-T1 — bounded transport with known target mixture

Assume for fixed b,c,o: (A1) target mechanism support is contained in measured source support; (A2) target mechanism proportions p_T(m) are known and nonnegative, sum to one; (A3) for each covered mechanism, |tau_T(m,o)-tau_S(m,o)| <= epsilon_m; (A4) the source estimator obeys |hat_tau_S(m,o)-tau_S(m,o)| <= eta_m. Then

    |tau_T(o) - sum_m p_T(m) hat_tau_S(m,o)|
        <= sum_m p_T(m)(epsilon_m+eta_m).

Proof: condition on m using the law of total expectation, add and subtract each source effect, apply the triangle inequality and sum the nonnegative masses. A finite-sample uncertainty claim requires a justified eta_m (e.g. randomization/cluster-aware bound) — it is never auto-generated by the arithmetic checker. epsilon_m is the substantive cross-ecology invariance restriction; inputting 0 does not establish it. **This is a conditional inequality, not identification from P69.**

**Important limitation:** If the target treatment changes the distribution of the mechanism itself, this formula is not a controlled natural-effect transport law; define m as a pre-treatment task feature and treat mechanism composition as fixed by sampling design. For signed effects the natural numerical domain is [-1,1].

## Theorem P70-T2 — nonidentification from source plus target anchors

Take one mechanism, a source identity response success probability 1/2 and source operator probability 3/4. In both target worlds, observed target identity is 1/2. The unobserved target operator success is 3/4 in world A and 1/4 in world B. All observed source data and target anchors coincide; target effects are +1/4 and -1/4. Therefore the target operator-effect sign is **not identified** without further cross-environment restrictions or target interventions. This counterexample is constructive and does not depend on a model call.

**Consequence:** Lexical/graph isomorphism, valid oracle answers, matched number of facts and an identical benchmark schema are each insufficient alone to identify an effect. A structural bridge is **necessary for a proposed semantic equivalence interpretation**, but cannot alone establish a statistical behavioral invariance claim.

## P70-T4 — Format selection as an independent observation axis

Let F indicate a response that satisfies the exact output contract and C indicate strict evidence-grounded correctness **only where F=1**. The all-call executable outcome is X = 1{F=1 and C=1}. For any observed model/provider/channel/task block with at least one valid response, write f=P(F=1), q=P(C=1|F=1) and x=P(X=1)=f q. Comparing arms a and b, the **exact descriptive identity**

    x_a - x_b = (f_a - f_b) q_b + f_a (q_a - q_b)

separates a format-selection component from the conditional-grounding component. This decomposition depends on reference arm b. It is an arithmetic accounting identity, **not** a causal separation (especially since C is undefined for invalid outputs and conditional samples are selected).

For a **hypothetical** completion of semantically missing format-invalid rows, an arm with n requests, v valid-format replies and k strictly correct valid replies admits an unconstrained completion interval [k/n,(k+n-v)/n]. These bounds are not observations of a latent fact and cannot turn invalid JSON into identified correct/incorrect content. An a-b completed effect spans [k_a/n_a-(k_b+n_b-v_b)/n_b,(k_a+n_a-v_a)/n_a-k_b/n_b]. Missing provider responses are likewise unconstrained rather than silently scored semantically false.

Apply only as **frozen historical finite-ledger arithmetic** to P69 #37925719478:
- Groq strict vs JSON: 48/48 vs 34/48 format-valid, 36/48 vs 25/48 all-call strictly grounded. Observed executable difference = +11/48 for strict. Hypothetical missing-format completion difference ranges -3/48 to +11/48, including zero. The exact reference-b decomposition assigns almost all executable difference to the format-validity change, while the remaining conditional-grounding difference is much smaller. This does **not** prove a format-mediated causal effect.
- Gemini strict vs JSON: 48/48 vs 47/48 format-valid, 26/48 vs 23/48 grounded. Observed executable difference = +3/48 for strict. The completion sensitivity range is +2/48 to +3/48; the much larger problem remains the JSON positive-valid abstention measured within the task classes, not just one invalid JSON.
- Both are paired task allocations with distinct draws/provider responses, not exchangeable independent mechanism clusters. Neither is evidence for or against ecological transport.

This motivated **two different P69-R1 repairs**: enumerated exact-output guidance and a separate evidence-tracing discipline. Preserve answer-direction and grounded-witness endpoints rather than retrofitting the scorer. `src/p70_selection_bounds.py` verifies exact rational decompositions and sensitivity intervals with zero calls.
