## 4. The fatal 'close alternatives ≠ realized future' distinction

Tambolo's case review warns that plausibility of a historical counterfactual is not a license to assume its distant scientific endpoint. For LEP:
- a short September extension **actually occurred**;
- a 2001 extension was **proposed but declined**;
- the 2003 reanalysis **actually occurred**;
- what a hypothetical 2001 run would have measured **did not occur**, and its outcome is not identifiable from the 2003 summary.

Kinzel's taxonomy further forces precision about alternative **apparatus scheduling** versus alternative **empirically successful scientific theory**. They are not the same type of alternative. The W13 observational-equivalence example is at most a pedagogical illustration of this distinction, not evidence of a historical gate.
