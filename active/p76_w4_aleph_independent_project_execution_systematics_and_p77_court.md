Original phase produced inflated sigma0~54.9 nb due to an extra detector acceptance correction. The recovery note reports paper-authenticated definitions and a corrected 42.18 nb with published efficiency; pipeline used 42.48 nb with within-fiducial MC efficiency, near final ~42.47. The project's self-documentation discloses data-vs-published selection, mapping of 1993 scan/pre-scan luminosity rows, removal of circular implied-efficiency, use of free per-year normalization nuisances and partial archive representation. The leave-one-out 1993 peak anchor removal gives a ~−430 MeV Gamma_Z swing and ndf 0, a severe design vulnerability; other 5 points are much less sensitive. This is a strong reason to reject 'fully replicated original ALEPH precision measurement'.
