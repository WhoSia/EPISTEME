## 2. Four candidate practices examined from *already-held* originals

### Case A — Breznau et al. (2022), real identical starting scientific question

[Held original PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1lsyHjglet1NaXlOf2EYfNPtW92jU5aYK/view) · [published PNAS article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9636921/) · [research code and survey workflows](https://github.com/nbreznau/CRI).

**Actual substantive hypothesis H:** increased immigration reduces public support for social policies. The comparison is H (negative relation) against not-H / no evidence / non-testability judgment. Researchers could use additional data for predictor operationalization; do NOT falsely call every input file and analytic choice literally identical. **161 researchers, 73 teams** received a shared starting survey frame and research question. **One team** found the hypothesis untestable and stopped after preliminary measurement tests. **72 teams** submitted **1,261 candidate statistical models**; **71 teams**, **1,253 converged models** yielded numeric estimates, and some teams offered dual judgments for distinct measures (89 judgment units vs 73 teams). Distinguish these denominators; never call all 1,261 models converged.

**W19 power:** This is an actual field-real, many-analyst case of divergent inference under a shared question, with public code and recorded decisions. It is strong evidence that inferential variety exists before any AI translation intervention.

**Fatal gap for EPISTEME:** There was **no assigned AI adapter, no standard-switching treatment, no measured change in community platform adoption, no randomized conversion-vs-control design**. Difference among statistical choices does **not** identify a translation-induced loss of scientific tests.

### Case B — Pritchard & Wicenec (2025), real implemented workflow comparator

[Held original PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1x_uwIkmcoWHC6NGEWEVMH9J82JSLZnsk/view); *Future Generation Computer Systems* 166:107684, DOI [10.1016/j.future.2024.107684](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.future.2024.107684). Authors develop **seven formally articulated reproducibility tenets** and hash-graph workflow signatures implemented in DALiuGE (motivated by SKA astronomy). Their demonstrative workflow **synthetically generates a sine wave, adds Gaussian noise, then compares lowpass filtering** through a NumPy pointwise baseline and CPU FFT / FFTW / CUDA FFT approaches. They compare filter outputs and workflow signatures; small numerical differences can coexist with operational/scientific reproducibility as defined.

**W19 power:** A genuine implemented experiment compares four computational methods and reproducibility/precision, directly killing the simplistic claim that a new EPISTEME 'test-path equivalence' concept is needed.

**Gap:** A sine-with-added-noise demonstrator is *not* a recorded new astronomical hypothesis H0/H1 being discriminated, nor an AI adapter's causal effect on an organization choosing a standard. Cannot inflate a computational benchmark into new astrophysical evidence.

## 4. Identification grid — do not silently concatenate unrelated papers

For a proposed causal effect of scientific-community AI translation, we need on the **same target unit and time interval**:

1. concrete scientifically discriminating H0/H1 and explicit observable/test statistic;
2. authentic fixed raw evidence and documented analytic workflow;
3. observed, assigned or otherwise identificationally credible **AI converter intervention** and a non-AI/manual or trusted converter control;
4. measured individual switching costs, community standard adoption/retention and feasible scientific test options (NOT just chosen outputs);
5. rival account controls: endogenous provision and network economics (Farrell–Saloner, Liu, Arthur, Heinrich, Rosero Abad), experimental epistemology (Blanco Sequeiros, Reijula, Bakeev), workflow provenance (PROV-IDEA, RO-Crate, Pritchard);
6. transparent direction of causal identification, uncertainty, error, selection, resources and time ordering.

**W19 outcome:** None of the four examined source cases individually satisfies all six. **Do not infer from the fact that separate studies cover different parts of the chain that their combination proves a new phenomenon.** There is a *research-design question* left, not a verified explanatory novelty.
