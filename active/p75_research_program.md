## 0. Founding-question lock
In a digitally standardized knowledge infrastructure, what makes a *better* representation adoptable when changing its schema, retained evidence, authorized historical version, interface, or proof role changes the valid routes of falsification? The estimand is not simply response correctness. It is the effect of the **representation under its warrant-preserving controls** on an actual **KEEP / SWITCH / REVIEW** decision, after legitimate source, claim, time and action costs. Hypothesis must beat competent standard provenance/temporal-query theory + established decision theory, not a weak latest-document or parser-only baseline.

**Claim ceiling:** We have no real human-approved warrant contrast and no independently measured switching utility. Historical hosted P69/P70 responses are genuine but are not measurements of the P75 policy effect. P75-P0 is offline precommit and negative-control code only.

## 4. P75-P1 next attack — one prospective claim-level discriminator
Unit is an independently published claim cluster, NOT response, prompt or reviewer. Prospective test asks whether a *warrant-preserving* representation change produces a **different, externally validated, decision-relevant action** beyond a maximally credible time+PROV+robust decision baseline.

- **Input blocks:** matched valid recoverable witness / matched missing witness / null witness, with P18/P19 matched source ID and trace placement; contract × full/projection × temporal claim validity; decoy citation and wrong-scope negative controls.
- **Outputs kept disjoint:** valid JSON; response content/class; source/evidence-ID minimality; actual independent proof-role and source-time assessment; signed human decision confidence/disagreement; policy KEEP/SWITCH/REVIEW under stated costs.
- **Controls:** independent source-custodian originals and snapshot SHA, predeclared claim-time and institution-authority, sealed gold from raters who did NOT author packets, no leakage of future source versions, reviewer randomization/balancing and cluster-aware inference.
- **Strong rivals:** SQL system/valid time + PROV+proof-role, classical minimax regret/stopping, Blackwell experiment comparison under known informativeness, network/coordination switching friction.
- **Reject novel mechanism** if high-grade conventional model matches P75 on both externally adjudicated warrant AND action, or if selection gains vanish under correctly matched recoverable witness and balanced review. A weak latest-publication baseline alone is insufficient for publication.
- **Scientific stop:** if no independent authority/source-time witness or blinded human custody is available, implement only source-count/packing and fail-closed validator, not another toy experiment to inflate PASS count.
