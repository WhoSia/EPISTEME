# EPISTEME-P69 — Manuscript Contribution and Falsification Gate

## Paper hypothesis, not a known result
After semantically invariant changes to archive presentation, operator-specific behavioral effects may depend qualitatively on task mechanism. A historical effect field fitted to one group of tasks may not predict a new mechanism. This is NOT a general claim that all prompt sensitivity is intrinsic or that representations never transport.

## Established historical baseline
- P56: 64/64 completion-world invariance, 0/6 primary Holm rejections, three-task stopping-time task×representation share 63.83–64.36%. These are descriptive shares in one specified tensor.
- P59: 4/4 completion invariance, 0/15 held-out transport tests and 16 sign-reversal observations. Reversal is a recorded effect under its task/replicate definition, not a proof of cross-model generality.
- P62: 256/256 logical completions reject the predeclared record/state framing transport law. P65: 4/4 reject the strong structural-location sign-transport branch. Neither rejects all possible context- or location-based mechanisms.
- P66 raw provider failures: 835/12288. Do not turn them into semantic errors, and do not claim a causal decoder intervention without P67's unexecuted experiment.

## Six indispensable rival explanations
1. Scorer artifacts: inspect raw outputs and independent semantic scoring; Hua et al. EMNLP 2025 already demonstrate this rival.
2. Stochastic hosted inference: independently repeated calls, explicit sampling settings and non-pseudoreplicated inference; stochastic prompt evaluation is established prior art (ReliableEval).
3. Mere lexical template effects: independently generated latent task mechanisms, not cosmetic skins of one relation.
4. Model or provider idiosyncrasy: two independent model/provider bundles and separate family-wise reporting; no model identity substitution after results.
5. Overfit interaction geometry: truly held-out task-mechanism prediction, not reconstruction on a known cube; task-independent baseline must compete.
6. Construct drift: each R/T edit must preserve a separately generated hidden oracle answer; no scientific effect from mismatched task semantics.

## Prospective design and budget — NOT YET EXECUTABLE

The initial instrument pilot is **192**, not 96, calls: 2 × 2 × 4 × 2 × 2 × 3. This arithmetic correction was recorded before any provider call. Provider-free 16-vertex pair audit is in src/p69_paper_preflight.py.

## Manuscript promotion
Promote only if held-out task-family prediction and reversal findings survive independent semantic scoring, format-failure accounting and a second model bundle. If a corrected scorer removes the effect, report an evaluation-artifact finding instead of intrinsic task sensitivity. If a held-out basis predicts the new task family, revise historical nontransport rather than relabel it. If evidence is inconclusive, do not turn nonsignificance into proof of the null.
