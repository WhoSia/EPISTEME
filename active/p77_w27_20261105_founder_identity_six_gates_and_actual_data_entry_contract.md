## 3. Formal *data entry contract* to prevent a paper-shaped invention

The intended EPISTEME estimand requires an observed or well-controlled contrast for `Z→A→T`, where:

- `Z`: defined AI translation *intervention* (model/provider, prompt, version, parser, error checks, time-cost and documentation), against a **best-known official or independently audited deterministic/manual baseline**, with common resources;
- `A`: community scientific standard adoption/retention measured across laboratories or a genuinely interaction-capable cohort and sufficient times, not a one-person logit preference or static cross-sectional share;
- `T`: independently validated set of H0/H1 *scientifically discriminating* tests (not availability of byte-conversion commands, statistical-significance colors, or mere numerical equality); include semantics, provenance, uncertainty and any trusted alternative route.

**At minimum to initiate a small technical pilot** (not the full chain): one genuine fixed scientific H0/H1 dataset with rights, two accessible named formats/toolchains, a trusted reference result, one established non-AI converter, executable tests and the *same validation budget*. No existing CRI-era record provides this intervention; it cannot be reconstructed by posthoc coloring.

**To prove the stronger *community lock-in* chain:** additional real adoption/choice/event logs or a credible group-assigned intervention with network interaction control, time order and error/independence assumptions. Interference makes naive per-person independent-treatment ATE inadequate. Human-participant recruitment would need the correct school/university ethics/authorization process; no recruitment has occurred. There may be no already-downloadable data satisfying this definition.
