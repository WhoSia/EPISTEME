## P71 exact decision distinction

Let d in [L,U] be SWITCH minus RETAIN net utility, migration cost already deducted. Robust dominance licenses SWITCH only if L>0, RETAIN only if U<0. In classical minimax regret, worst-case SWITCH regret is max(0,-L), and RETAIN regret max(0,U). For [-1/5,3/5], minimax regret chooses SWITCH but robust dominance is HOLD. These are distinct objectives, not inconsistent results. This elementary result belongs to established decision theory, not a new EPISTEME theorem.

## Audit-induced observational equivalence

Define A=1 for publicly performed audit and A=0 for unaudited operation. In synthetic W+ and W- both observed audited net benefits are d(1)=1/4. The unseen counterfactual is d(0)=1/4 in W+ and d(0)=-1/4 in W-. The audit-only observations are identical; unaudited optimal migration decisions differ. This is a basic positivity/identification counterexample, NOT empirical evidence that audits changed P69/P70 target outcomes.

Conditional sensitivity: if an independently supported bound |d(1)-d(0)|<=delta is available, original [L,U] widens to [L-delta,U+delta] under audit. Choosing delta after observing outcomes or assuming delta=0 simply because the same text was shown invalidates the claim. The exact-rational finite counterworld suite is in src/p71_endogenous_audit_court.py.
